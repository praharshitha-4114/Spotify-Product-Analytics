# Spotify Product Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview

The Spotify Product Intelligence Platform is designed to analyze user behavior, music streaming activity, subscription performance, and business KPIs.

This project simulates the work of a Product Data Analyst at Spotify.

The platform will help answer important business questions related to user engagement, revenue, customer retention, and listening trends.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze user listening behavior.
- Track subscription growth.
- Monitor revenue trends.
- Identify customer churn.
- Discover popular artists and songs.
- Improve user engagement.

---

## Stakeholders

- Product Managers
- Business Analysts
- Marketing Team
- Finance Team
- Executive Leadership

---

## Expected Outcome

Build a complete analytics platform using PostgreSQL, SQL, Python, and Power BI.
